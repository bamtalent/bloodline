About the Game:
BLOODLINE uses roguelite permadeath and weighted probability mechanics as an interactive lens into how systemic barriers—from the transatlantic slave trade and the 1884 Freedman's Bank collapse to the 1921 Tulsa Race Massacre, federal redlining, COINTELPRO, and the War on Drugs—compound across generations.
